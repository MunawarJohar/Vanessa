Rescheduling and Cancellation Policy

Rescheduling

Each student is eligible for a ONE-TIME discounted reschedule opportunity of $40 off any missed course, or for any reschedule requested within 2 business days of when the course starts. This allows students who experience scheduling conflicts, technical issues, or other unforeseen circumstances to complete their training without paying the full course fee again.

Cancellations

Students who cancel at least two (2) business days prior to the scheduled course may request a refund less a $15 processing fee. This fee helps offset non-refundable payment processing and administrative costs.

Cancellations made less than two (2) business days before the scheduled course are not eligible for a refund but may qualify for a reschedule as previously outlined.
