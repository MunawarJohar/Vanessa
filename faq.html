Your Questions,
Answered

Who needs this training?+

Licensed childcare providers in California who need to fulfill the 8-hour Preventive Health and Safety training requirement, including Nutrition and Lead Poisoning Prevention.

How do I sign up?+

Pick an available class date from the schedule on the home page and select "Book." You'll complete payment to confirm your spot.

How much does the course cost?+

The 8-hour course is $95.00, whether it runs as a single all-day session or split across two shorter sessions.

How fast do I get my certificate?+

Certificates are delivered digitally the next business day after you complete the course.

What topics are covered?+

Infection control policies and immunization laws, injury prevention, nutrition, lead poisoning prevention, ADA laws for childcare programs, quality care in early childhood education, and cultural awareness in a childcare program.

Is this course CA-EMSA approved?+

Yes. This course is CA-EMSA approved and accepted statewide for childcare licensing.

How long is the course?+

The course totals 8 hours, offered either as one full-day session or as two 4-hour sessions on consecutive days.

Is the class online?+

Yes, all classes are live and held online via Zoom.

Does this certificate expire?+

Check with your licensing program analyst for renewal timing, as requirements can vary by program type.

Is this the same as CPR/First Aid?+

No. This is a separate Preventive Health and Safety training. CPR and First Aid training is offered separately — see the Resources page for details.

What are the cancellation and rescheduling policies?+

See the full Rescheduling and Cancellation Policy page for details on refunds and the one-time discounted reschedule option.
